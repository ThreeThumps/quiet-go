Rules used here

You choose the scoring for each new game. Japanese (territory) scoring counts the empty points you surround plus every stone you captured, including dead stones removed at the end. Chinese (area) scoring counts your stones on the board plus the empty points you surround. White gets komi to make up for moving second.

Suicide is not allowed, and a single-stone ko can't be retaken immediately. With Japanese scoring, eyes inside a seki are counted as territory here, a small simplification.

The game ends after both players pass in a row. The computer then guesses which stones are dead. Tap a group to switch it between dead and alive, then accept the score.

Fine-tune

Personality sliders shape which moves the computer likes. On levels 6–10 it still plays a clearly better move when it sees one, and uses its personality to choose between moves that are about equally good.
